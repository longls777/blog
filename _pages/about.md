I graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Information Engineering from [BUPT](https://www.bupt.edu.cn/) and am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence, focusing on large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs). Previously, I have interned at ByteDance, Didi, and Alibaba Group.
